About This Pilot Program

The SAP Ariba Catalog Pilot Program is an initiative by SAP Ariba Network Services to expand catalog coverage for specialized industrial equipment categories. Phase 1 focuses on industrial sensors, acoustic monitoring devices, and related automation components. Supplier onboarding follows the standard Ariba Network qualification workflow, ensuring all catalog entries meet enterprise procurement compliance requirements.

This portal currently operates in limited-access pilot mode. Additional product categories and supplier listings will be added progressively as the program scales toward full production deployment.
