Product Description

The SR-9000 Industrial Sonic Radar is a long-range acoustic sensing device designed for industrial monitoring, warehouse automation, perimeter detection, and object localization applications. The EU variant (SR-9000-EU) is manufactured in Germany by SonarDynamics GmbH and features enhanced environmental protection ratings suitable for outdoor deployment in harsh conditions.

Key Features

  • Detection range: up to 120 meters in open environments
  • Operating frequency: 20 kHz – 200 kHz configurable sweep
  • IP67 rated enclosure for outdoor industrial deployment
  • Integrated Modbus TCP/IP and OPC UA connectivity
  • 36-month manufacturer warranty with on-site support (EMEA)

Supplier Information

SonarDynamics GmbH is a German manufacturer specializing in industrial acoustic sensing and ultrasonic measurement systems. Founded in 2011, the company serves customers in manufacturing, logistics, and infrastructure sectors across Europe and Asia-Pacific.
