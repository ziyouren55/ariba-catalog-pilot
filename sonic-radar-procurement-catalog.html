Long Description

The SR-9000 Industrial Sonic Radar (EU variant) is a long-range acoustic sensing and localization device engineered for demanding industrial environments. Designed and manufactured by SonarDynamics GmbH at their facility in Stuttgart, Germany, the SR-9000-EU offers a detection range of up to 120 meters with configurable frequency sweep between 20 kHz and 200 kHz. The unit features an IP67-rated enclosure, integrated Modbus TCP/IP and OPC UA communication interfaces, and is certified for ATEX Zone 2 hazardous area deployment.

Supplier Profile

SonarDynamics GmbH is a specialist manufacturer of industrial acoustic sensing and ultrasonic measurement systems headquartered in Stuttgart, Germany. Established in 2011, the company serves over 400 enterprise customers across manufacturing, logistics, energy, and infrastructure sectors in Europe and Asia-Pacific. SonarDynamics maintains ISO 9001:2015 certification and operates a dedicated R&D center focused on next-generation acoustic sensing technologies.
