Product Description (Official)

The SR-9000 Industrial Sonic Radar (EU variant, SKU: SR-9000-EU) is a long-range acoustic sensing and localization device engineered for demanding industrial environments. Designed and manufactured by SonarDynamics GmbH at their ISO 9001:2015 certified production facility in Stuttgart, Germany, the SR-9000-EU delivers a detection range of up to 120 meters with configurable frequency sweep between 20 kHz and 200 kHz.

The device features an IP67-rated enclosure for full dust and water ingress protection, integrated Modbus TCP/IP and OPC UA communication interfaces for seamless industrial network integration, and is certified for ATEX Zone 2 hazardous area deployment. Typical applications include warehouse automation, industrial perimeter monitoring, logistics object detection, and acoustic localization in manufacturing environments.
