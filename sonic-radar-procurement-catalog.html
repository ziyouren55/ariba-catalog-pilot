Product Description

The SR-9000 Industrial Sonic Radar (EU variant, SKU: SR-9000-EU) is a long-range acoustic sensing and localization device specified for demanding industrial environments. The catalog record documents design and manufacture by SonarDynamics GmbH at an ISO 9001:2015 certified production facility in Stuttgart, Germany, with a detection range of up to 120 meters and a configurable frequency sweep between 20 kHz and 200 kHz.

The catalog entry records IP67 enclosure rating for full dust and water ingress protection, integrated Modbus TCP/IP and OPC UA communication interfaces, and ATEX Zone 2 certification for hazardous area deployment. Documented application areas include warehouse automation, industrial perimeter monitoring, logistics object detection, and acoustic localization in manufacturing environments.
